Roofing

Barking Frog Roofing is a locally owned exterior contractor serving DMV with roofing, siding and gutter work done right the first time. We handle everything from full roof replacements and storm damage repairs to new siding, soffit and fascia, and seamless gutters that keep water away from your home. Every project starts with a free, honest inspection and a clear written estimate, and we’ll help you work through insurance claims when storms hit. Our crews show up when we say we will, protect your property while we work, and clean up completely before we leave. Every job is backed by our 10-year workmanship warranty.

Siding

Barking Frog Roofing installs, replaces and repairs siding to protect your home and give it a fresh, finished look. We work with a range of popular materials, including vinyl, fiber cement and engineered wood, and we’ll help you choose the style, color and profile that suit your home and budget. Whether you need a full siding replacement, repairs after storm damage, or updated soffit, fascia and trim, our crews handle every step with care. Each project starts with a thorough inspection for hidden moisture or rot behind the old siding, so your new exterior is sound underneath as well as good-looking on the outside. We finish every job with a complete cleanup and a final walkthrough to make sure you’re happy with the result.

Our story

Founded in 1998 by Bernie Saundersun, Barking Frog Roofing was created when he asked his two year old son what sound a frog makes and he started barking. At that moment, he knew it was fate, he had to start a roofing company with a barking frog. Over the years around the DMV, Barking Frog Roofing has become famous for its eclectic advertisements on both TV and radio.

Every customer gets delicate care of their property, and all the dogs we meet along the way will always get a treat because they are such good boys (and girls). We love and respect our customers and everyone in their family, and flawless work is what we strive for.
